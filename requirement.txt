# =========================
# Vendor Store Management
# =========================

- The system shall allow each vendor to create and manage a dedicated online store.
- Each vendor store shall have a unique store name that cannot be duplicated by another vendor.
- The unique store name shall serve as the store subdomain for client access.
- The system shall validate store names during creation to ensure uniqueness.
- Vendors shall be able to publish their stores to make them publicly accessible.
- Vendors shall be able to unpublish their stores to temporarily disable public access.

# =========================
# Product Management
# =========================

- Vendors shall be able to create, edit, and delete products.
- Vendors shall be able to publish products to make them visible to clients.
- Vendors shall be able to unpublish products without deleting them.
- Each product shall contain:
  - Product name
  - Product price
  - Product category
  - Product description
  - Product image
- Vendors shall be able to assign products to categories.

# =========================
# Storefront Customization
# =========================

- Vendors shall be able to customize their client-facing storefront.
- Vendors shall be able to upload and manage store banners.
- Vendors shall be able to customize storefront font styles.
- Vendors shall be able to customize background colors.
- Vendors shall be able to customize foreground colors.

# =========================
# Client Store Access
# =========================

- Clients shall be able to visit individual vendor stores using store subdomains.
- Clients shall be able to browse available products.
- Clients shall be able to view detailed product information.

# =========================
# Shopping Cart and Checkout
# =========================

- Clients shall be able to add products to a shopping cart.
- Clients shall be able to remove products from the shopping cart.
- Clients shall be able to modify product quantities in the cart.
- The system shall calculate the total cart value.
- Clients shall be able to proceed to checkout and complete purchases.
